Xerophager converts the single-codepoint PNG images into deterministic bitmap
font atlases. ASCII keycap bases, the combining keycap mark, regional-indicator
flags, and private-use placeholders are excluded because Minecraft 1.21.1's
bitmap font provider does not perform Unicode sequence shaping.
